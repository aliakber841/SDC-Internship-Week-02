Our Story

In Every Jar

WATCH

WE STARTED HEARTHGLOW BECAUSE MOST CANDLES WE OWNED SMELLED GREAT FOR A WEEK, THEN FADED.

In 2022, we began pouring candles in a rented kitchen studio, testing burn times on our own dining table until a jar could hold its scent all the way to the bottom.

Today we're still a small team — no factory, no outsourced pouring. Every candle that ships still passes through the same three pairs of hands it did on day one.

What We Stand For

Three things guide every decision we make, from the wax we choose to the box it ships in.

Honest Ingredients

100% natural soy wax, cotton or FSC-certified wood wicks, and phthalate-free fragrance oils. No paraffin, no shortcuts, no exceptions.

Small Batches

We pour in batches of 40 at a time, so every jar gets tested for scent throw and burn quality before it's approved to sell.

Slower Packaging

Reused glass jars, recyclable boxes, and soy-based ink on every label — because a candle about slowing down shouldn't arrive wrapped in plastic.

THE STUDIO TODAY

We don't scale fast.

We'd rather stay small enough to answer your emails ourselves.

Every order is still packed by hand in our studio. If a wick tunnels or a jar arrives cracked, we hear about it directly and we fix it directly — not through a support ticket queue.
