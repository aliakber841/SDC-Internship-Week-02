OUR CRAFT

We don't chase trends.

We pour candles the slow way, in small batches, so every jar burns the way it's meant to.

Every candle uses natural soy wax, cotton or wood wicks, and phthalate-free fragrance oils. We test every batch for burn time and scent throw before it ever reaches your door — because a candle that tunnels or fades halfway through isn't good enough for our shelf.

PRIVACY POLICY

Hearthglow is a small, independent candle studio. Every candle is hand-poured in-house, in batches small enough that we can taste-test the room, not just the wax. We're always working on using less plastic in our packaging and more reused glass in our jars — it's a slow process, but so is a good candle.
